# Geographic Imbalance in Spatial Generalisation of Water Quality

<p align="center">
  <img src="ICML_POSTER_SUSHMA_kumari.jpg" alt="ICML Poster" width="900"/>
</p>

Global lake water quality monitoring via satellite remote sensing is critical for tracking eutrophication, harmful algal blooms, and drinking water safety. ML models trained on the **GLORIA** dataset (n = 6,675 in-situ samples) promise global applicability — but are they truly generalisable?
